How it works

From lens to action in five steps

A continuous pipeline connects field devices to the central server — every detection becomes owner information, and every policy match becomes an action.

01

Capture

Endpoint cameras, mobile cameras and other devices photograph passing vehicles.

02

Detect & read

AI locates the plate in the frame; OCR reads the number, day or night.

03

Match owner

The number is matched in the database to retrieve owner & registration details.

04

Check policies

Rogue lists, insurance, expiry dates, toll rules and emergency status are checked.

05

Alert & act

Alerts fire, notices go out, signals change, tolls reconcile — automatically.

Core capabilities

Four modules, colour-coded like the road itself

Red for threats · amber for compliance · green for emergencies · blue for infrastructure — each maps to a policy the admin controls.

Rogue plate alerts

Flag criminal & wanted vehicles

Admins maintain a watchlist of criminal / rogue plates. Any camera that reads a listed plate triggers an instant alert with location, time and captured evidence.

Compliance & notifications

Insurance & expiry verification

Configurable checks verify insurance status and registration / permit expiry as vehicles pass. Owners and drivers get automatic notifications before documents lapse.

Green corridor

Priority for emergency vehicles

Registered ambulances and emergency vehicles get automatic green signals when on duty. Drivers toggle duty mode in a linked mobile app connected to the server.

Toll integration

Works at toll-collecting nakas

The same detection pipeline plugs into toll plazas — identifying vehicles at the barrier, supporting collection workflows and applying every policy check at the naka.

The connected ecosystem

Devices on the road, one server behind them

Fixed and mobile capture points stream to the central server; the driver app closes the loop for emergency fleets.

Endpoint cameras

Fixed cameras on gantries, junctions and toll nakas capture continuously and stream detections to the server.

Mobile cameras

Patrol vehicles and handheld devices extend coverage anywhere — the same OCR and policy checks, on the move.

Driver duty app

Emergency drivers switch duty mode on and off; the server grants green signals only when it matters.
